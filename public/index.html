ABOUT ROLE RADAR

Less searching.
More moving.

Role Radar uses TinyFish Search to discover live openings, TinyFish Fetch to read those pages, and TinyFish Agent for optional interactive verification. Results are deduplicated, scored against your preferences, and linked to their source for applying.
